About

Dr. Siqi Huang is an Assistant Professor in the Academy of Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Technology (AIAT) at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU), Entrepreneur College (Taicang). He received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of North Carolina at Charlotte in 2022, and his B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Sun Yat-sen University in 2015.

His primary research interests encompass Edge Computing with Embedded AI Devices (TinyML), Computer Vision and Deep Learning, Vision LLMs & AIGC, Mobile Augmented Reality (MAR), and Autonomous Driving Systems. He serves as Programme Director for BEng Data Science and Big Data Technology with Contemporary Entrepreneurialism, and heads the XJTLU Metaverse Research Lab.

Research Interests

Edge Computing & TinyML

Resource-hungry applications on embedded AI devices; cloud-edge offloading and scheduling.

Computer Vision & Deep Learning

Image restoration, dehazing, object detection, and compression frameworks for UHD video.

Vision LLMs & AIGC

LLM-powered translation, layout-to-image augmentation, and generative pipelines.

Mobile Augmented Reality

Real-time open-set detection and smart-decision systems for mobile AR applications.

Autonomous Driving

Lane detection, in-car voice assistants, and vehicular telematics for connected vehicles.
